Oregon Adult Foster Home — Resident and Provider Agreement

Important: This is a customizable draft. The provider must ensure that the final agreement complies with the applicable Oregon Administrative Rules, the resident's person-centered service plan, Medicaid requirements when applicable, and current Oregon Department of Human Services requirements.

2. Admission and Agreement Term

This Agreement describes the residential services, care, supports, rates, policies, responsibilities, and rights applicable to the resident's stay at the Adult Foster Home.

3. Services and Supports

The AFH will provide services and supports according to the resident's assessed needs, person-centered service plan, care plan, applicable orders, and applicable Oregon requirements.

4. Rates, Charges, and Payment

Payment Source

Rate Changes

For non-Medicaid residents, applicable Oregon rules require written notice before general rate increases or modifications. Rate changes associated with increased care or service needs may be handled according to the agreed rate schedule and applicable rules.

5. Refund and Payment Policy

Any refund and payment policy must comply with applicable Oregon rules. Unused advance payments will be handled according to applicable requirements.

9. Meals, Food Access, and Dietary Needs

Residents must have appropriate access to food consistent with applicable resident rights and their individual needs.

10. Medication and Health Support

Medication administration and health-related services will be provided only within the scope authorized by applicable law, orders, training, and the resident's care plan.

11. Personal Belongings and Storage

The resident is not responsible for damage resulting from normal wear and tear to the AFH or its contents.

12. Visitors, Privacy, and Personal Choice

The AFH will not establish visitor restrictions that conflict with applicable resident rights or HCBS requirements.

13. Monitoring Devices and Technology

Agreement to home monitoring devices is not a condition of admission. Any monitoring arrangement must comply with applicable law, resident rights, privacy requirements, and informed consent requirements.

Voluntary Move

Involuntary Move / Discharge

Any involuntary transfer or discharge must comply with applicable Oregon requirements, resident rights, notice requirements, and applicable administrative hearing or appeal rights.

15. Complaints, Grievances, and Appeals

A resident may exercise applicable complaint, grievance, reporting, appeal, and administrative hearing rights without unlawful retaliation.

16. Emergency Procedures

17. Resident and Provider Acknowledgment

By signing this Agreement, the parties acknowledge that they have reviewed the terms of residency, including the services to be provided, applicable rates and charges, house policies, refund policies, resident rights, and move/transfer provisions.

The resident and/or resident representative has had an opportunity to ask questions and request clarification regarding this Agreement.

Nothing in this Agreement is intended to waive a resident's legal rights, limit a provider's liability for negligence, or create a condition that is prohibited by applicable Oregon law or administrative rules.

18. Signatures

The Agreement must be signed by the applicable parties before admission. Each party should receive a copy of the signed Agreement.
